RentSight Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

RentSight estimates the total cost of rental listings. Listing analysis uses local preliminary extraction and, for signed-in RentSight+ accounts, automatic AI review through RentSight's backend and OpenAI. Account and billing requests are sent to RentSight's API and its payment processor.

Information RentSight handles

After you click the RentSight toolbar icon, the extension reads rental listing content from the active page. It may store the page URL, property address and listing details, your entered financial assumptions, saved comparisons, preferences, and cached regional estimates in Chrome's local extension storage.

A cloud-enabled build persists Firebase Authentication state and may handle your Firebase user ID, profile name, email address, subscription status, and billing-flow metadata. Firebase ID tokens are obtained when needed for authenticated requests; RentSight does not create or store a custom RentSight JWT. Payment card details are entered on Stripe-hosted pages and are not handled by the extension.

How information is used and shared

For AI review, captured listing text, relevant structured data, the listing URL, selected unit, and preliminary extracted values are sent to RentSight's backend and OpenAI. Customer overrides and household financial assumptions are not included in extraction requests. Accepted extraction results, evidence, and notes are stored with local drafts and saved listings; full captures are held temporarily for the request and retries, not saved locally. Account and subscription information is used to authenticate users, determine feature access, and provide billing through Stripe. RentSight does not sell information or use it for advertising or analytics.

Storage, exports, and deletion

Data remains in the current Chrome profile until you delete it, remove the extension, or clear the browser's extension data. You can delete individual saved listings or use About & Privacy → Delete all local data. Files you explicitly export are saved wherever your browser puts downloads and may contain addresses and financial assumptions.

Permissions

RentSight uses temporary access to the active tab only after you invoke it, local storage for the features described above, scripting to inspect that selected page, Chrome's side panel to display the interface, and narrow network access to the RentSight API origin and configured Firebase authentication hosts. The offscreen permission supports the Google sign-in flow required for Manifest V3. When you choose an assisted Zillow or Apartments.com search, RentSight separately asks for optional access to that site so it can apply supported filters through the site's own search controls. This access is limited to the chosen rental site and is not required for other sites. RentSight does not request access to every website you visit.

Changes

If RentSight's data practices change, this policy and the extension's disclosures will be updated before the changed practices take effect.
